Modification Rules

Healthy Modifications
Sodium: Reduce soy sauce 25-30%, always low-sodium. Aromatics carry flavor without salt.
Sugar: Reduce 30-50% in sauces. Use natural sweetness from onion, carrot, ripe fruit.
Oil: 1 tsp where recipe says 1 tbsp. Air fry or bake instead of deep fry.
Protein boost: Add tofu, edamame, or egg to vegetable dishes to hit 120-140g daily.
Hair goals: Prioritize salmon, eggs, spinach, lentils, pumpkin seeds, shiitake.
99 Ranch greens: A choy, gai lan, ong choy, pea shoots, napa cabbage, baby bok choy.
Baking default: Bake chicken, salmon, vegetables when possible — less oil, easy cleanup.
Drinks: Never add sugar — use ripe fruit, cold brew, or coconut water's natural sweetness.
